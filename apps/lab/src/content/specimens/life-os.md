Life OS is a cross-platform AI app built with Flutter and Supabase, aimed at giving people a single place to plan, track, and reflect on daily life instead of juggling separate apps for tasks, habits, and journaling.

The AI layer sits across the app rather than bolted onto one screen — it's meant to help interpret what's going on in a day and suggest what to do with it.

This is the flagship project in the lab: the most ambitious build, and the clearest example of shipping something end to end across mobile, backend, and AI.
