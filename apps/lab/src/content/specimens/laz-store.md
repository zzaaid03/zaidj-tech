LAZ Store is an Android e-commerce app for Tesla parts, built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose. Its standout feature is AI-assisted parts identification: point the camera at a part and the app helps identify what it is and whether it's in the catalog.

Because it's a native Android app, it can't run directly in a browser. The demo on this page is a recreation of the parts-identification flow rather than the live app itself.
